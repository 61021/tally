# Identity

Picked from rendered tastings on 30/9/2026, 1/10/2026 and 3/10/2026.

- **Name:** Tally. Wordmark set in Newsreader italic (`app/components/TallyWordmark.vue`).
- **Type:** Newsreader for the wordmark, headings and sentences (may change to another serif later). Geist for everything you click or scan: buttons, labels, settings, metadata. Geist Mono for every digit on the board, pad, timers and stats. All three self-hosted by `@nuxt/fonts`. Geist is the base face; `h1` and `h2` take Newsreader in `app/assets/css/main.css`, and a sentence opts in with `font-serif`.
- **Direction:** minimal and clean with an old storybook-cartoon warmth: a painted world with an inked UI on top. The painting carries the warmth, and everything you read or press is a flat, solid surface laid over it.
- **Scenes:** four public-domain paintings by Theodor Kittelsen, each with its own colors sampled from the painting: Dusk (_Andersnatten_), Soria Moria (_Soria Moria_, 1900, the default), Haystacks (_Kornstaur i måneskinn_) and Marsh (_The Twelve Wild Ducks_, 1897). Picked from a tasting of storybook plates, sources in `app/constants/appearance.ts`. Tokens in `app/assets/css/themes.css`, paintings in `public/scenes` (AVIF at 1280, 1920 and 2560 wide, a JPEG fallback and a thumbnail).
- **Modes:** System, Light, Dark, OLED, Dark by default. System follows the OS between light and dark, never OLED. Dark shares one night palette across the scenes (Marsh keeps its own green) over the painting as it is. OLED drops the painting: true black page, near-black surfaces.
- **Backdrop:** `app/components/SceneBackdrop.vue`, fixed behind every page. Calm bands at the top and bottom take the headings and the footer, the only text set on the painting itself, and that text is always in `--ink`, never `--muted`. Everything else sits on a cel: `bg-surface` with the `cel` shadow. Settings, sign-in and account each sit on one sheet, and the game panel is a card beside the board.
- **Board:** the Moonrise grid with a solid cell background and "whisper" lines: cell lines at 5% ink, box lines at 16%, one rounded 10px frame. The selected cell fills with `--ink` (near-white on dark, near-black on light), its digit or notes knocked out in `--surface`, and turns `--error` red when its digit is wrong; cells with the same digit take a neutral fill (ink 10%), the row, column and box a faint band (ink 4%), hover ink 6%. No circles. Every change fades over 90ms. Given digits are Geist Mono 400, the player's are 300 in the set's `--user` color.
- **Finish:** the board thins out and a moon (the accent color) rises behind it.
- **Word wall:** behind the whole hub, every country Atlas asks about, in Newsreader capitals (12px, 0.16em tracking) at 7 to 14% ink, with the cards laid over it. The mouse leaves a 0.9s trail that lights words within 110px in the accent (mixed 55% with ink in light modes, where the gold alone washes out). The heading, the top bar's links and the footer keep clear of it. It turns on for a mouse only, from the media query or the first mouse move, since Firefox can report a touchscreen laptop as having no fine pointer; under reduced motion only the words under the pointer light, with no trail. `app/components/WordWall.vue`.
- **Atlas screen:** the map is the whole screen on every device, fitted to the screen's shape with the land each map ships around its region filling the rest, so nothing is letterboxed. On a laptop the question floats over its top as one cel (region and progress, "Where is" and the country or the result, the score and the one button) and the round's results over its right side, with the rail over its left edge. On a phone a slim bar at the top carries back, region, score and settings, and the question sits at the bottom in thumb reach with the results stacked above it. Scroll or pinch to zoom up to 6x, drag to move, + - 0 on the keyboard, and a corner button fits the region again; a reveal outside a zoomed view backs out.
- **Atlas map:** the board's language on a map. Sea is the surface, land ink 14%, borders ink 34%, meridians and parallels every 15° at the cell-line color, one rounded 10px frame. A pin is an ink cross; the country it was meant for fills with the accent, a dashed line runs to its nearest edge, countries already asked keep an accent tint, and a country too small to see gets a ring once asked. World is drawn in Equal Earth, each continent in Lambert azimuthal equal-area around its centre.
- **Atlas data:** Natural Earth 10m in its Saudi Arabia worldview, so Israel's land is drawn as Palestine and the Golan inside Syria (it also draws Western Sahara inside Morocco). Northern Cyprus, Somaliland and Baikonur draw with Cyprus, Somalia and Kazakhstan, and disputed land no country covers (Abyei) is plain land nobody is asked about. The 194 asked countries are the UN's members and observers in `shared/atlas/countries.ts`; `vpr atlas` regenerates `public/atlas`, drawing the land around each region (enough for an ultrawide laptop and a portrait phone, Antarctica included) coarser the farther it lies from the region.
- **Page changes:** instant, with no transition. A puzzle opened from inside the app loads before the page changes, so its board arrives whole.
- **Contrast:** every scene passes WCAG AA in all three modes: body text 12:1 on the surface, muted and the player's digits 4.7:1 on both the page and the surface, accent fills 4.5:1 with their label. Text on the painting is measured against the pixels behind it, at the laptop and phone sizes.

## Tokens

Each scene defines eight per mode: `--bg`, `--surface`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--on-accent`, `--user`, `--error`. Everything else (lines, peer highlight, the soft accent) derives from them in `app/assets/css/main.css`, which also maps them onto Nuxt UI's `--ui-*` variables.

The chosen scene and mode live in the `tally-appearance` cookie, so the server renders `data-scene` and `data-mode` on `<html>` and the right painting, and nothing flashes on load. A cookie from the color-set days keeps its mode and gets the default scene.
